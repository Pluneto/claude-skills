# Memorable dashboards: examples

Source: a Mobbin video analysis of ~2,108 dashboards (observations from the video, not verified independently). Use for analogy, not as rules.

## Insight + next action
- **Fey** (stock detail): news-summary panel sits right beside the price chart, so the chart comes with its interpretation.
- **Origin** (finance): "Your weekly recap" as a swipeable story-style card and a "Daily market brief" card in the right rail; easy to digest.

## Signature visual encoding
- **Shopify Live View**: globe of dots, each a real visitor right now, with cards for sessions, orders, locations, and a cart/checkout/purchased funnel.
- **Front**: busiest-times heat map (day x hour, darker = busier) instead of a table or line chart.
- **Cake**: equity ownership donut with a literal birthday cake in the center (consumer-friendly playfulness on low-stakes data).
- **Visitors** (web analytics): stands out by being very clean in a field of busy dashboards (restraint as the differentiator).

## Delight in empty/success states
- **Mercury**: after bills are paid, the empty "zero inbox" table becomes a small Space-Invaders-style game. Consumer-leaning; only on a low-stakes state.

## Principle
The memorable ones weren't the prettiest, they knew what to leave out and made the data instantly understandable.
